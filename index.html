Drop Of Hope – Smart Water Management System

How Drop Of Hope works

Press the button to see the system respond to a leak.

  1. Sensors collect readings.
  2. ESP32 processes the data.
  3. The system detects abnormal readings.
  4. The relay controls the solenoid valve.
  5. The valve can shut off water flow.
  6. The mobile app receives an alert.
